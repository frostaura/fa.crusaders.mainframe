# MEMORY — Technologies/projects/crusaders.mainframe

- [Red — the relay question is an electrical safety matter, and it is unanswered](memory/safety-relays.md) — RED: nobody has established whether the court relays switch a contactor coil or the lighting load directly. If direct, an electrician is needed before anything ships — no software decision touches this, and it gates go-live.
- [Current state](memory/state.md) — PR #2 is merged and main publishes three images; PR #3 (door gates) is an OPEN DRAFT; claude/eco-mail carries the email redesign locally with NO upstream and nothing pushed. Nothing deployed, nothing on hardware.
- [Live decisions — product and economics](memory/decisions.md) — Product and economics: blueprint Rev 2 is the customer's word and outranks internal specs; credits are the unit of account; charge AT BOOKING supersedes charge-on-lights-on and still needs committee sign-off; PayFast replaced Peach.
- [Live decisions — what a board may tell the club about itself](memory/decisions-device-alerts.md) — How a board reports on itself: one outage mail per cooldown derived from the rows, silence about any board no admin has acknowledged, a cap of 16 unacknowledged rows answered 409, and a door that is never a court's evidence.
- [Live decisions — the device contract and firmware](memory/decisions-device.md) — The device contract is hobby-grade by founder decision — one per-tenant API key, auto-registration, no OTA or discovery; the brains are on the ESP8266 because the Mega cannot be flashed over WiFi; the wiring is fixed and untouchable.
- [Live decisions — the door gate's cadence and its refusals](memory/decisions-door-gate-cadence.md) — How often the door gate speaks and what a refusal costs it: the 15 s floor under a flapping link, the scan bound that covers both uploads, and what a 403 or a 404 on each device path does to the roster and the queue.
- [Live decisions — the door gate's HTTP call](memory/decisions-door-gate-http.md) — One HTTP call on the door gate: the framing the real server sends and how G2's fix made every call time out, the three phases and their budgets, and the two since-boot counters the board has nowhere else to put.
- [Live decisions — the door gate's edges](memory/decisions-door-gate-io.md) — The door gate's edges: a compiler-checked API_BASE_URL that refuses the example file's own placeholder, every secrets value guarded, what a UART overrun costs, and the Arduino-IDE flash route with its version trap.
- [Live decisions — the door gate's pass](memory/decisions-door-gate-loop.md) — The door gate's pass and what shares its one blocking call: why the loop left main.cpp, the load-bearing step order, at most one call with the 15 s watchdog relation and its lamp fault, and the true release-overrun bound.
- [Live decisions — the door gate](memory/decisions-door-gate.md) — One pull-only ESP32 per door; 200 and 403 are the only authoritative answers, all else falls back to the roster; LOCKED is asserted before the pin drives; what the door does with no power is unmeasured — the owner expects it released.
- [Live decisions — the admin doors console](memory/decisions-doors-console.md) — The admin doors surface: one vocabulary for four audit actions, a deleted door that is removed rather than absent, an edit that says which one it was, signals with nowhere to put a member, and reopen-all as an undo.
- [Live decisions — the club door log, its day and its export](memory/decisions-doors-log.md) — The club door log and its export: the club's day computed on the server, a half-open range built at midnight's own offset, clamped to retention, no member field in the request at all, and a CSV that defuses before it quotes.
- [Live decisions — the member's doors, and what is deleted](memory/decisions-doors-member.md) — A member's own door history is P3's other half and lives only on /me/door, filtered by tenant AND subject because neither catches what the other does; and the door trail is now deleted past the club's retention rather than anonymised.
- [Live decisions — what the club's mail is, and what it may claim](memory/decisions-email.md) — Every message is sent by a CLUB, not by the platform: one identity resolver, both bodies derived from one block list so they cannot drift, the registration mail merged to match what the screen promises, and no image anywhere.
- [Live decisions — who a door opens for](memory/decisions-gate-access.md) — Who a door opens for: a booking code bounded by its own booking, capped at four hours and clamped at every reader; NoShow and Completed both count; admins pass online only; 403 can mean nothing but out of service.
- [Live decisions — the door's threat posture](memory/decisions-gate-threat.md) — What the door's posture actually claims: a cleartext LAN, bearer-token codes, provisioning that hands out other members' codes on demand, and an audit trail that stopped being a credential store.
- [Live decisions — the door gate's shape](memory/decisions-gate.md) — The door gate reuses the court box's contract with no EF migration; a board declares its kind once, event kinds are normalised once, a door is never attendance, and its trail finally has a reader.
- [Live decisions — how quality is proven here](memory/decisions-method.md) — Quality here is proven by refutation, not by a green suite: every fixer is followed by an agent paid to disprove it, rounds repeat until two consecutive ones find nothing high, and parallel lanes are scoped by FILE, never by theme.
- [Live decisions — settlement and the ledger](memory/decisions-settlement.md) — How the ledger settles: a no-show releases its tail by truncating EndsAt, late box evidence reverses it with two NEW rows (never an edit, balance may go negative), the resold tail is displaced, offline scans judged at scan time.
- [The launch bar](memory/launch-bar.md) — The working set of conditions agreed on 2026-07-24 for calling Crusaders launched — bookings, lights-on match rate, positive balances, second-club onboarding time, uptime — and the fact that nothing instruments any of them.
- [Gotchas — the test suite and its fixtures](memory/gotchas.md) — The test suite lies in specific ways: no fixture read a real socket, an EmailMessage is not a message, prose against a wrapped text part is green by luck, in-memory EF proves the opposite. Clock traps live in gotchas-clock.
- [Gotchas — the firmware toolchain](memory/gotchas-build.md) — Toolchain traps in both trees: src/iot is several PlatformIO projects on one venv, every arduino/ tree is GENERATED, both cores force gnu++11, GCC under UBSan will not fold an object address in a constexpr — and on the .NET side make complexity is unrunnable as written, lizard mis-parses expression-bodied switch members into a file-spanning *global*, and a stale migration Designer snapshot makes every later migration re-add columns.
- [Gotchas — the wall clock as a hidden test input](memory/gotchas-clock.md) — Tests whose hidden input is the wall clock: red in the last half-hour of a UTC day, the door evening red for an hour after a seeded booking, AddHours(3) red every afternoon, and failures this Mac cannot reproduce by construction.
- [Gotchas — this machine and this iCloud checkout](memory/gotchas-env.md) — This checkout sits on iCloud: MSBuild is denied copying appsettings into bin, iCloud fabricates numbered duplicates inside generated sketches, and a worktree under .claude/ inherits a hidden flag that makes .NET ignore appsettings.json.
- [Gotchas — frontend, vitest and the browser harness](memory/gotchas-frontend.md) — React and browser-harness traps: renderPage(<StrictMode>…) is INERT so lanes wrote tests they believed were guarded, user-event's keyboard path hangs under jsdom 28, and only the repo's own Chromium can register a service worker.
- [Gotchas — the quality gates and their lint tooling](memory/gotchas-gates.md) — Gates that report a red run as green or trip on a function nobody wrote: complexity exits 1 on stdout, lizard's C# and .tsx lexers mis-scope (one form of it no single member causes — split the file), ls-files misses untracked work.
- [Gotchas — the shared JSON scanner](memory/gotchas-json-scanner.md) — devapi's heapless JSON scanner treated any quoted string as a key until it was fixed; every server-controlled string is a decoy for a key name, and the flat any-depth scan is depended on and must not be narrowed.
- [Gotchas — club-local instants and the database](memory/gotchas-postgres-offsets.md) — Npgsql refuses any DateTimeOffset with a non-zero offset on a timestamptz parameter, so a club-local instant thrown at a query is a 500 for every club outside UTC while every UTC fixture stays green.
- [Open questions](memory/questions.md) — All founder-owned and none of them technical: PIN length (a 4-digit collision charges the wrong member), biometric vendor SDK, gate policy, revoked-card window, pricing beyond Crusaders, and whether the club goes live this quarter.
- [Watch list](memory/watch.md) — Unpaid debts, worst first: .env holds live JWT, database, SMTP and device secrets unencrypted and unbacked-up; the redeploy is manual and nothing checks the stack before it; charge-at-booking is unsigned-off.
- [Watch list — the doors](memory/watch-doors.md) — What the door gates leave owed: no-power behaviour unmeasured and two facts owed by Carl, no gate has run on hardware, a court box's roster is an unnamed door key, and everything that is NOT a door row still grows for ever.
